## 1. Scenario
A hypothetical e-commerce retailer uses a third-party hosted payment page, requiring consideration of systems that could affect checkout security.

## 2. My role and contribution
Prepared a proposed payment flow, preliminary scoping analysis, twelve-domain assessment plan, evidence requests, and hypothetical remediation examples.

## 3. What I produced
- [Payment architecture](01-architecture/payment-flow.md)
- [Scoping approach](02-scope/scope-assessment.md)
- [Evidence register](04-evidence/evidence-register.md)
- [Executive report](06-final-report/executive-report.md)

## 4. Decisions and reasoning
Treated checkout redirection and payment-page influence as scoping concerns instead of assuming all merchant web systems are out of PCI DSS scope.

## 5. Results and verification
The linked deliverables are available for review in this repository. No system validation, scanning, segmentation test, evidence verification, third-party assurance review, or actual PCI assessment was completed. Compliance status remains undetermined.

## 6. What I would do next
With real organizational authorization, validate scope and assumptions with stakeholders, collect actual evidence, run appropriate tests, document exceptions, and seek accountable approval before any production rollout or closure claim.

## 7. Skills demonstrated
Security governance, risk assessment, evidence traceability, clear documentation, stakeholder-ready reporting, and separating proposed controls from verified results.
